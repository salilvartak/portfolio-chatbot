# Fun Facts and Life Outside Work

## Music
Salil is a big fan of Hindi/Bollywood music. His taste leans soulful and slow (Arijit Singh, Shashwat Sachdev, Sachet–Parampara, indie Hindi artists), and he carefully curates his own Spotify playlists.
He even built himself a Spotify player: a lyrics-first web player with kinetic typography, because he wanted the lyrics to be the main show.

## Gaming and the game he is making

He's making a game set in a 1930s noir city, with rubber-hose cartoon-style character portraits.
